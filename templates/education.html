Basics

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers trick users into giving sensitive information like passwords, credit card details, or personal data by pretending to be a trusted source.

Types

Types of Phishing

Email Phishing

Fake emails pretending to be from trusted companies like banks or services.

Most Common

URL Phishing

Fake websites designed to steal your login credentials and personal data.

High Risk

Smishing

Phishing attacks delivered through SMS text messages on your phone.

Growing

Vishing

Voice call scams pretending to be officials from banks or government.

Dangerous
Identification

How to Identify Phishing

  • Check Sender Address

    Look carefully at the sender's email domain for any misspellings

  • Hover Over Links

    Always hover over links before clicking to see the actual URL

  • Urgency Language

    Be suspicious of emails creating urgency or threatening consequences

  • Grammar Mistakes

    Poor spelling and grammar are common signs of phishing emails

  • Never Share Passwords

    Legitimate companies will never ask for your password via email
